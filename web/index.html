Questions people ask

Do I need any experience?

No. Every course starts from nothing and assumes you haven't done this before. If a term needs explaining, the lesson explains it.

How much time does it take?

Lessons are short enough to do in a sitting. A module opens each week, so most people spend a few evenings a week on it rather than a whole weekend.

What if I pick the wrong chapter?

Switch whenever you like. Pro opens all sixteen courses, and whatever you've finished stays finished.

Can I cancel?

Yes, in one tap from your account page. You keep Pro until the period you've paid for ends, and anything you've already finished stays open to you.

Is this a get-rich-quick thing?

No. It teaches how these businesses actually work — pricing, clients, contracts, delivery, money. Nobody can promise what you'll earn, and we don't.
