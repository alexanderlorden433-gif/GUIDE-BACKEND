Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

1. What this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains what information Alexander Lorden ("The Guide," "we," "us") collects when you use our website and application (the "Service"), how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. Information we collect

Account information. When you sign up, we collect your email address and a password (stored as a securely hashed value — we never store or have access to your actual password).

Business data you enter. The Service is designed for you to track your own business activity, including (depending on which features you use): client names and details, quotes, invoices, expenses, business goals, notes, reminders, and similar records you choose to enter. This data is yours — see Section 5 for how we handle it.

Payment information. If you subscribe to a paid plan, payment is processed by Stripe, our third-party payment processor. We receive confirmation that a payment succeeded and basic billing status, but we do not receive or store your full card number.

Usage information. We may collect basic technical information automatically, such as browser type, device type, and general usage patterns (e.g., which features are used), to help us maintain and improve the Service.

We do not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us so we can delete it.

3. How we use information

We use the information we collect to: - Provide and maintain the Service, including saving your progress and business data across sessions - Process payments and manage subscriptions - Send account-related communications (e.g., password reset emails) - Respond to support requests - Maintain the security and integrity of the Service - Improve the Service based on aggregate, non-identifying usage patterns

We do not sell your personal information or your business data to third parties. We do not use your data to serve you third-party advertising.

4. Who we share information with

We share information only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers who help us operate the Service, such as our database/hosting provider and Stripe for payment processing. These providers are only given the access needed to perform their function and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes.
  • Legal requirements. We may disclose information if required by law, subpoena, or other legal process, or if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others.
  • Business transfers. If we're involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction; we'll provide notice before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

We do not otherwise share your account information or business data with third parties without your consent.

5. Your business data

Client names, invoices, expenses, and other business records you enter are stored so the Service can function (that's the point of the product). You can export a full copy of your data at any time (Stats → Back up everything), and you can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from Account Settings. Account deletion is immediate and cannot be undone — we don't retain a copy after you delete it, except where we're legally required to retain limited records (e.g., billing records for tax/accounting compliance).

6. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including password hashing and standard access controls on our infrastructure. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

7. Cookies and local storage

The Service uses browser local storage to keep you logged in and remember basic preferences (like dark mode). We do not currently use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your personal information, or to object to certain processing. You can exercise most of these directly in the app: - Access/export: Stats → Back up everything (JSON) - Correction: update your account details or business data directly in the app - Deletion: Account Settings → Delete account

For anything not available directly in the app, contact us at TheGuide1@gmail.com.

Users in the EU, UK, or California may have additional rights under GDPR or CCPA/CPRA; if you are located in one of these regions and have questions about your specific rights, please contact us at TheGuide1@gmail.com.

9. Data retention

We retain your account and business data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your data is deleted immediately except where we're required to retain certain records (such as billing history) for legal or tax compliance purposes, which we retain only as long as legally required.

10. International data transfers

Our servers are located in the United States. If you are accessing the Service from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do, and for material changes, we'll provide more prominent notice.

12. Contact us

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your data can be sent to TheGuide1@gmail.com.
