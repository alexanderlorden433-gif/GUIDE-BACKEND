Hourly rate calculator

Work backward from the income you want to the hourly rate you need. Free, no signup: add your goal, costs and billable hours.

How it works

Your rate has to cover three things: the money you want to keep, what the business costs to run, and the tax on what's left. The calculator adds those up and divides by the hours you can actually bill.

Billable hours are the hours a client pays for. Quoting, messages, driving, marketing and bookkeeping are real work too, but nobody pays for them directly, which is why your rate usually comes out higher than you'd expect. Most people starting out bill fewer hours than they plan.

Treat the number as your floor. If it's far above what people near you charge, look at the costs and hours first, then at offering packages instead of hours. The tax figure is a rough set-aside, not tax advice; rules depend on where you live.
