Margin and markup are not the same

Margin is the share of the price you keep: charge $150 with $60 of costs and you keep $90, a 60% margin. Markup is how much you add on top of cost: $90 on top of $60 is a 150% markup. Mixing them up is one of the most common pricing mistakes, and it usually means charging too little.

Count every cost that comes with the sale: payment fees, packaging, shipping, gas, supplies, the share of your ads it took to get the order. If you leave them out, the margin looks healthy on paper and disappears in practice.
